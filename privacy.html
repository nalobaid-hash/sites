Privacy Policy

We collect only what is needed to deliver the service: your email address, your order details, and the material you send us.

How we use it

To deliver and support your order and to send the service you subscribed to. We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Processing

Bank transfers go directly to the seller's bank account; we never see or store card details (where a Tap Payments payment link is used, Tap Payments handles the card data). Documents you send are processed with AI-assisted tools and reviewed by us for the sole purpose of delivering your order. We do not use your documents to train models.

Retention

Order files are deleted 30 days after delivery. Email correspondence is kept for support and accounting purposes.

Your rights

Email us to access, correct or delete your data at any time. Subscribers can cancel from any email or by replying to one.
